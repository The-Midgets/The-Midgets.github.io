About The Midgits

Welcome to the chaotic, comical world of The Midgits! We are a cartoon about a hilarious band, their bossy, upright pig, Pansy, and a dog named Nacho who just wants so nap all day, we bring you music, mayhem, and a lot of funny piano sounds.

Meet the Band

Max

Accordion & Leader
Gets curious, causes chaos, and squeezes the accordion until it wheezes.

Penny

Recorder
Her recorder squeaks so loud it usually makes Nacho howl.

Petunia

Drums
Wild, loud, and always ready to start a cartoon fight.

Pansy

Piano & Manager
The upright pig who trys to keep the band on track (and plays the funny piano).

Nacho

The Dog
Doesn't talk, just howls in tune, chases runaway sounds, and rolls his eyes at the camera.

How It's Made

Animation: Everything is modeled and animated in Blender.

Music: The soundtrack is recorded using a real accordion, recorder, piano, and drums. Yes, the funny piano sound is real!
